1. **Ankunft am rosa Schloss**
   Lisi, her big sister Nora and their dad (Papa) walk up a gravel path toward Drumlanrig Castle in Scotland at dusk – a huge pink sandstone castle with many small turrets and a curved double staircase. Dozens of glowing jack-o'-lanterns line the stairs. Lisi stops and looks up in wonder while Nora hops over puddles.

2. **Morag und die Pergamentrolle**
   At the castle door, a friendly woman in a long green hooded cloak hands Lisi and Nora a parchment scroll tied with a black ribbon. Pumpkins glow around them, both girls eagerly raise their hands.

3. **Das Herz mit Flügeln**
   Inside a grand hall with dark oil portraits and a sparkling chandelier, Nora points excitedly at a carved stone heart with wings above an archway. An old man in a tartan cap lights a candle nearby, Lisi looks up amazed.

4. **Im Saal der Ahnen**
   Nora lies flat on her tummy and Lisi crouches next to her on a wooden floor in a long, dim portrait gallery, both discovering a tiny winged heart carved on a chair leg. Stern old dukes and duchesses in wigs and lace collars watch from the walls; pumpkins glow on the windowsills. An old wooden chest with a number lock sits in the corner.

5. **Die Wendeltreppe**
   Lisi and Nora climb a narrow stone spiral staircase hand in hand, lit by small lanterns, their big shadows dancing on the wall. Lisi goes first, curious; Nora behind her, a little nervous.

6. **Das Eichhörnchen-Gespenst**
   A tower bedroom with a big four-poster bed with dark red curtains. On the windowsill sits a red squirrel with tufted ears holding a hazelnut, tapping on the glass. Lisi and Nora stand in the doorway, giggling.

7. **Der Riesenkürbis**
   View from a tower window at night: a garden full of hundreds of glowing jack-o'-lanterns like orange stars, and one gigantic grinning pumpkin the size of a wheelbarrow next to a fountain. Lisi and Nora press their noses to the glass.

8. **Der Schatz**
   Lisi and Nora kneel on the lawn next to the giant pumpkin; Nora holds the chest while Lisi opens a small wooden chest with an old rusty key. Inside: a bag of Scottish fudge, two little bat-shaped flashlights and two brass pins with a winged heart. Morag approaches with a lantern.

9. **Heimweg**
   Lisi, Nora and Papa walk away along the gravel path under a starry sky, the pink castle behind them. Brass winged-heart pins shine on Lisi's yellow scarf and Nora's tartan scarf. In one tower window, the silhouette of a small bushy-tailed squirrel.
