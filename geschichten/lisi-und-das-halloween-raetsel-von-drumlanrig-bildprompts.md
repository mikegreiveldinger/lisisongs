# Bild-Prompts: Lisi und das Halloween-Rätsel von Drumlanrig Castle

Zum Einfügen in ChatGPT. Am besten zuerst das Lisi-Cover (gelbes Bild aus „Lisi fragt nach") hochladen und dazuschreiben:
„Das ist Lisi. Bitte halte sie in allen folgenden Bildern genau so: gleiches Gesicht, gleiche Mütze, gleicher Schal, gleicher Malstil."

**Stil-Anker (an jeden Prompt anhängen):**
> Warm children's book illustration, soft gouache and watercolor texture, gentle paper grain, cozy and slightly spooky but never scary. Lisi: a girl of about 6 with light brown hair in a low ponytail and bangs, big green eyes, rosy cheeks, a mustard-yellow knitted beanie with a big pompom and a thick mustard-yellow knitted scarf. Square format, no text.

---

1. **Ankunft am rosa Schloss**
   Lisi and her dad walk up a gravel path toward Drumlanrig Castle in Scotland at dusk – a huge pink sandstone castle with many small turrets and a curved double staircase. Dozens of glowing jack-o'-lanterns line the stairs. Lisi stops and looks up in wonder.

2. **Morag und die Pergamentrolle**
   At the castle door, a friendly woman in a long green hooded cloak hands Lisi a parchment scroll tied with a black ribbon. Pumpkins glow around them, Lisi's hand is eagerly raised.

3. **Das Herz mit Flügeln**
   Inside a grand hall with dark oil portraits and a sparkling chandelier, Lisi points excitedly at a carved stone heart with wings above an archway. An old man in a tartan cap lights a candle nearby.

4. **Im Saal der Ahnen**
   Lisi crouches on a wooden floor in a long, dim portrait gallery, counting a tiny winged heart carved on a chair leg. Stern old dukes and duchesses in wigs and lace collars watch from the walls; pumpkins glow on the windowsills. An old wooden chest with a number lock sits in the corner.

5. **Die Wendeltreppe**
   Lisi climbs a narrow stone spiral staircase lit by small lanterns, her big shadow dancing on the wall. She looks up, curious and a little nervous, holding the handrail.

6. **Das Eichhörnchen-Gespenst**
   A tower bedroom with a big four-poster bed with dark red curtains. On the windowsill sits a red squirrel with tufted ears holding a hazelnut, tapping on the glass. Lisi stands in the doorway laughing.

7. **Der Riesenkürbis**
   View from a tower window at night: a garden full of hundreds of glowing jack-o'-lanterns like orange stars, and one gigantic grinning pumpkin the size of a wheelbarrow next to a fountain. Lisi presses her nose to the glass.

8. **Der Schatz**
   Lisi kneels on the lawn next to the giant pumpkin, opening a small wooden chest with an old rusty key. Inside: a bag of Scottish fudge, a little bat-shaped flashlight and a brass pin with a winged heart. Morag approaches with a lantern.

9. **Heimweg**
   Lisi and her dad walk away along the gravel path under a starry sky, the pink castle behind them. A brass winged-heart pin shines on Lisi's yellow scarf. In one tower window, the silhouette of a small bushy-tailed squirrel.
